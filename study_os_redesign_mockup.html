3.2 Mitosis and Cell Division
Mitosis is the process of nuclear division producing two genetically identical daughter cells, followed by cytokinesis.
The four stages of mitosis are prophase, metaphase, anaphase, and telophase.
During prophase, chromatin condenses into discrete chromosomes and the mitotic spindle begins to form.
